These entries come from CAFANS. The two main buttons open the CAFANS homepage, and each product name opens its listing. Check agent compatibility before ordering.

What should I check in warehouse photos?

Compare the color, size label, measurements and visible condition with the option you ordered. Ask about exchanges before the return window closes.

Why can shipping quotes change?

The destination, shipping line and packed parcel dimensions can change the quote. Ask whether the line bills by actual or volumetric weight and check which fees are included.
